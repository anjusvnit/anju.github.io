About

Dr. Anju is an Assistant Professor Grade II at Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology (SVNIT), Surat. Her academic background is in Micro-Nano Electronics and VLSI, with research interests including semiconductor device physics, emerging nanoscale devices, low-power memory devices and biosensors.
